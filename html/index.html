Everything the front desk and pharmacy need

Six connected pages, each built for one job.

Patients

Keep names, phone numbers and dates of birth in one register, and see how many visits each patient has had.

Appointments

Book a patient into a time slot. The system refuses double bookings, and you can mark visits completed or cancelled.

Prescriptions

Write a prescription with the drug, dosage and quantity, then dispense it with one click.

Stock

Record every drug with its quantity, price and reorder level. Low items are flagged so you restock in time.

Sales

Record over-the-counter and prescription sales. Totals and revenue update as you go.

Expiry dates

See what has expired and what expires within 30 days, and write off expired units so they are never sold.

How the pages work together

Entering information once updates every page that needs it.

BookRegister the patient and book their appointment.
PrescribeWrite the prescription from the drugs currently in stock.
DispenseOne click removes the units from stock and records the sale.
MonitorThe dashboard shows low stock and expiring drugs at a glance.
